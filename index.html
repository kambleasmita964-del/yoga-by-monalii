About Yoga

Yoga is an ancient practice that connects the mind and body. It includes physical postures, breathing exercises and meditation. Regular suitable practice can support flexibility, balance and relaxation.


Our Mission: To spread yoga awareness and encourage people to include safe yoga practices in daily life.

Benefits of Yoga

Mental Wellness

Breathing exercises and meditation can help manage everyday stress.

Flexibility

Gentle yoga movements can improve flexibility and mobility.

Fitness

Regular practice supports general fitness and well-being.

Relaxation

Relaxation techniques can help calm the body and mind.

Balance

Standing poses can develop balance and body awareness.

Concentration

Mindful breathing can support attention and focus.

Yoga Poses

Surya Namaskar

A sequence of yoga postures coordinated with breathing.

Tadasana

Mountain Pose encourages upright posture and balance.

Vrikshasana

Tree Pose is a standing balance posture.

Vajrasana

A kneeling seated posture. Avoid it if it causes discomfort.

Bhujangasana

Cobra Pose is a gentle backbend. Do not force the movement.

Shavasana

A relaxation posture practised while lying comfortably on the back.
